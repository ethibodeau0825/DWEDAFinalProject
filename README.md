# DWEDAFinalProject

This project is motivated by the need to explore how the voices of key stakeholders and other academic factors contribute and support school culture and academic expectations. 
Academic expectations refer to the benchmarks that schools believe students can and should achieve, and they play a critical role in shaping student outcomes. 
The findings may help schools and policymakers better understand the conditions that promote high academic standards and show areas of improvement for primary and secondary schools.
